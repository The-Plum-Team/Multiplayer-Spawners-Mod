# Update - 2.0.0
## New

* **__New versions support 1.21.2 - 26.3 Fabric / NeoForge__**

* **A new look for player-placed spawners**
> A spawner placed by a player behaves like a vanilla one: it spawns for everyone, breaks normally and doesn't put out torches. With a mob it now looks dark steel with blue eyes, so you can tell it from the golden world spawners at a glance.

* **Spawners can become active again**
> Server owners can make a disabled spawner active again for each player after a while with `spawnerRespawnMinutes` (0, the default, keeps them disabled for good) or `/multiplayerspawners respawn <minutes>`. Players are told when it will come back.
> *@Fjor thx for the idea!*

* **Option to turn off the chat messages**
> `showNotices` or `/multiplayerspawners toggle_notices` turns off the mod's informational chat messages.
> *@tyrthurey thx for the idea!*

* **Breaking spawners with spawner tools**
> With `breakWithSpawnerTools` or `/multiplayerspawners toggle_break_tools`, a tool of the `multiplayerspawners:breaks_spawners` item tag (Composite Material's dungeon pickaxe by default) breaks a spawner like vanilla, and its mod decides what drops. With the break link turned off as well, spawners can only be broken with those tools or in Creative.
> *@rednetwo thx for the idea!*

* **Single-use spawners for every player**
> Blocks that spawn once and then remove themselves can be added to the `multiplayerspawners:one_shot_spawners` block tag with a datapack: each player gets their own use, and once it's used up for you it stays for the others.

## Compatibility

* **Quark compatibility**
> The monster box no longer disappears for everyone after the first player triggers it. Each player gets their own: once it's used up for you, it shows in the vanilla spawner's colours and stops showing its particles to you, while it stays ready for the others.
> *@inf0214 and @vancomb thx for the idea!*

* **Apotheosis compatibility**
> The boss spawner no longer disappears after the first player triggers it: each player gets their own boss.
> *@inf0214 thx for the idea!*

* **Apothic Spawners compatibility**
> Spawners you disabled no longer keep spawning mobs, and spawners players place are recognised again.
> *@pray_star_jirachi thx for reporting it!*

* **Ageing Spawners compatibility**
> A spawner that runs out no longer disappears for the whole server: it runs out only for the players who used it, and stays for the rest.
> *@mochacat thx for reporting it!*

* **Brass Amber Battle Towers compatibility**
> Tower spawners work and break as the mod intends again.
> *@decxmber thx for reporting it!*

* **Respawning Structures compatibility**
> A regenerated spawner no longer looks disabled to players who had disabled the old one in the same spot.
> *@demivfx thx for reporting it!*

## Fixes

* **Nearby players are always disabled too**
> Group disabling now works on every hit. Before, hitting a spawner that someone else had already disabled could leave the players next to you with it still active.

* **No more flickering spawners**
> Hitting a disabled spawner no longer makes it vanish or flash for a moment, and a spawner you place no longer shows the active look before switching off. Empty spawners now always look off.

* **Spawners no longer spawn for players who disabled them**
> A spawner you disabled no longer spawns mobs when nobody who still has it active is near, or looks active again after you respawn.
> *@alejoo930_ thx for reporting it!*

* **Better performance on big servers**
> Thousands of spawners and torches take much less work, and joining, respawning and loading chunks no longer hit the server hard.

* **Unlit torches drop again on 1.21+**
> Breaking a torch that a spawner put out drops it again.
